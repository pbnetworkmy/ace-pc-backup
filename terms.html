Terms of use

Effective 24 October 2026

ACE-PC Backup is a personal utility for the PC owner's own folders and Google Drive account.

Authorized use

Use the application only for files and accounts you own or are authorized to manage. The owner chooses the source folders, approves Google access and controls the scheduled backup.

Backup behavior

The application uploads from the PC to Drive. It does not download Drive changes into the source folders or delete source files. Files removed from the PC remain in Drive until separately removed. The local copy may overwrite a changed Drive copy on a later run.

Availability and verification

Backup depends on the PC, Windows session, internet connection, available Google storage and valid Google authorization. Transfers can be delayed or fail. The owner should check transfer logs, verify important copies and keep other backups where needed. The application does not guarantee uninterrupted service or complete, permanent file history.

Account and credential security

Keep the Windows account, OAuth credentials and Google account secure. Google Drive sharing and storage settings remain under the owner's control. The application can be disconnected through the Google account's third-party connections.

Third-party services

The utility uses rclone and Google Drive. This public information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Each service remains subject to its own applicable terms. ACE-PC Backup is not a Google or GitHub product.

Privacy and support

Read the privacy policy for data handling and deletion details. For support, contact the application owner using the support email displayed in the Google authorization screen or your existing contact channel with the owner.
